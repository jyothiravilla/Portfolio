Firmware Engineering Intern

Jun – Aug 2026

Bausch + Lomb · St. Louis, MO

Designed closed-loop stepper motor control firmware on the TMC2160 driver for a solid-state illumination board, controlling the position of a surgical laser's iris mechanism with no physical limit switch available for end-of-travel detection.

Implemented a stall-detection homing routine using the driver's StallGuard load feedback to determine when the iris reached its mechanical end-of-travel. Without it, the motor continued driving after the iris hit its limit, causing the gear train to bind under continued torque. Tuned the stall threshold until it reliably triggered at the true limit without false-triggering during normal travel. Also authored SRS, architecture, and detailed design documentation for the Ileos glaucoma laser platform, and expanded automated test coverage for the upcoming Stellaris/Innova release.

Sensorless Stepper Homing

A stall-detection homing algorithm for a TMC2160-driven stepper motor controlling a surgical laser's iris mechanism. Without end-of-travel detection, the motor continued driving after the iris reached its mechanical limit, causing the gear train to bind. StallGuard load feedback let the firmware detect the stall condition and cut power at the true end-of-travel point.

Stack: Embedded C, TMC2160 (StallGuard), SPI
